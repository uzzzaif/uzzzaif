---

## How I Approach Problems

I don't jump to solutions. My process starts with understanding the system, look for what's broken, why it exists, and what a correct fix actually looks like.

- I break down complex problems into smaller, verifiable components before writing a single line of code.
- I debug methodically: isolate, hypothesize, test, and repeat. Not guess-and-check.
- When something doesn't work, I treat it as information, not failure. Some of my most valuable learning has come from hours spent tracing a single bug.
- I think in terms of tradeoffs: readability vs. performance, simplicity vs. flexibility, speed vs. correctness.

Frustration is part of the process. Patience and attention to detail are non-negotiable.
